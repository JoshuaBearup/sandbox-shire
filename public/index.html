Sandbox Shire

Sandbox Shire is a council that does not exist. Ava is the assistant on its website. Over three acts you take her hidden instructions, reach staff-only data through her, and rewrite what she tells every resident, using nothing but sentences.

It all runs on your own machine, against a model you pick.
