Breaking an AI assistant with natural language

Sandbox Shire

Sandbox Shire is a council that does not exist, with an online assistant called Ava who behaves exactly like the real ones. Over three acts you will take her hidden instructions, reach staff-only data through her, and then rewrite what she tells every resident — using nothing but sentences. No code, no tools, no exploits.

It all runs on your own machine, against a model you pick.

1 Connect a model

Checking what is configured…

Stored in config.local.json next to the server, readable only by you, and never sent to the browser again — not even partly. It is gitignored, so it cannot be committed by accident.

2 Change the difficulty

You do not need to touch this to play. The default is the model the workshop ran on and it works. But if you want it harder, change the model underneath Ava — one click, on the same OpenRouter key you have already set up.

Nothing else changes when you do. Same assistant, same instructions, same guardrail wording. Only the model moves, and the game gets harder. That is the part worth taking back to work.

3 Play

Three acts, in order. Each one hands you the key to the next, and Ava never gains a single new ability along the way — only your understanding of her does.
